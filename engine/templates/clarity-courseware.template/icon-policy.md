# Clarity Courseware Icon Policy

- Use `@phosphor-icons/react` only.
- Use one weight per slide: `light` by default, `regular` when small sizes need clarity.
- Icons support meaning in diagrams, process steps, and metadata. They are not
  decorative filler.
- Keep icons monochrome. Orange may identify one active or emphasized item.
- Provide a visible label or accessible name whenever an icon carries meaning.
- Do not mix Lucide, Material, Font Awesome, emoji, or custom brand marks.

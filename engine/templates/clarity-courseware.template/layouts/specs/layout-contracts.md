# Clarity Courseware Layout Contracts

## Global

- Fixed 1600×900 canvas, 12 columns, 8 rows, 64px frame, 24px gutters.
- White is the dominant/default background. Orange and black are pacing tools.
- Inter owns all structural typography. Newsreader may replace the first exact
  `highlightPhrase` inside one title, quote phrase, or concept callout.
- Keep one idea per slide. Use rules, alignment, and whitespace instead of cards.
- Corners stay sharp. No gradients, texture, glass, decorative grid, or copied
  brand assets.
- Header: course left, lesson right. Footer: section left, current/total right.
- Title cover and full-bleed image suppress footer; full-bleed image suppresses header.

## Density

- Lists: 3–5 items.
- Tables: 5 columns × 6 rows maximum.
- Code: 14 visible lines maximum.
- Image grids: 2–4 cells.
- Newsreader: never body, code, table, header, footer, or metadata.
